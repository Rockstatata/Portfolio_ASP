## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are recruiters, collaborators, and potential clients evaluating the portfolio owner for opportunities. Secondary users are peers checking projects, blogs, and technical skills. The main jobs are to assess credibility quickly, review work depth, and contact the owner with confidence.

### Brand Personality
Modern, elegant, professional. The interface should feel trustworthy and refined, with a confident technical tone and low visual noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean portfolio presentation inspired by Laravel-like typography and a crimson-accent visual identity. Light and dark modes are both first-class and should feel equally polished, with consistent surfaces, readable contrast, and subtle motion. Avoid clutter and avoid flashy effects unless they support hierarchy.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve a single visual language across light and dark themes.
2. Prefer design tokens and reusable patterns over one-off styling.
3. Keep hierarchy clear: strong headings, calm body text, restrained accents.
4. Make interactions obvious and accessible with visible focus states and consistent controls.
5. Keep admin workflows direct, fast, and minimally distracting.
